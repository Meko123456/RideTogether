# RideTogether privacy policy

_Last updated: 2 September 2026_

RideTogether is a group-ride app for small groups of motorcyclists. This policy describes exactly
what it collects, who sees it, and how long it lasts. It is short because the app collects little.

## What is collected

**Your location, while a ride is running.** Between the moment you tap **Start ride** or join a
ride and the moment that ride ends, RideTogether reads your position and shares it with the other
riders in the same ride. This is the app's entire purpose: it draws the group on a map and warns
the group if someone drops back or stops unexpectedly.

Location is **not** collected when no ride is running. There is no background collection: while a
ride is in progress the app runs a service with a notification you can see, and when the ride ends
the service stops and collection stops with it.

**A display name and an anonymous account identifier**, so other riders in your ride can tell who
is who.

**Ride events**, such as joining, leaving, and alerts raised during the ride, so the group has a
shared history of what happened.

## What is not collected

- No contacts, photos, files, messages, or call data.
- No advertising identifiers. There is no advertising in the app.
- No analytics or tracking SDKs.
- Nothing at all when you are not in a ride.

## Who sees it

Only the other riders in the same ride room, and the servers that pass the data between you
(Google Firebase). It is never sold, and it is never shared with anyone else.

## How long it is kept

A ride room and everything in it — positions, events, membership — expires **24 hours** after the
room was created, and is deleted then. Ending a ride stops collection immediately. Leaving a room
removes your positions from it.

## Your control

- Location sharing requires your permission, which you can refuse or withdraw at any time in
  Android's settings. The app remains usable without it; you simply will not appear on the map.
- Ending a ride, or leaving a room, stops sharing at once.
- Uninstalling the app removes everything held on the device.

## Children

RideTogether is not directed at children and should not be used by anyone under 16.
